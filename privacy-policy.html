Privacy Policy

Application: ChurchDesk

Last updated: 2026-03-13

This policy explains how ChurchDesk collects, uses, and protects user and device information.

ChurchDesk is designed to support church administration workflows, including member management and document exports. Privacy and data protection are considered in product design and daily operation.

1. Scope and Controller

This policy applies to personal and technical data processed through ChurchDesk mobile features. The app owner or organization operating ChurchDesk acts as the data controller for the information entered by users.

2. Information We Collect

  • User-provided data: member records, notes, and settings entered within the app.
  • Photos: only when the user chooses to select or capture profile images.
  • Technical device data: when applicable, for app functionality, diagnostics, and service improvement.

3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

  • Service delivery: process data to provide core app functionality requested by users.
  • Legitimate interest: improve reliability, security, and performance of the app.
  • Consent: camera/photo access is used only after user action and permission.
  • Legal obligations: data may be processed or disclosed when required by applicable law.

4. How We Use Information

  • Operate and maintain app functionality.
  • Store and display records securely for the user.
  • Generate reports and exports requested by the user.
  • Diagnose issues and improve stability and performance.

5. Device Permissions

  • Camera: to capture profile photos when requested by the user.
  • Gallery/Files: to select images or share/export documents.
  • Internet: for network features and content delivery when applicable.

6. Storage, Retention, and Security

  • Reasonable security measures are applied to protect information.
  • Sensitive data is handled using technical controls aligned with the app architecture.
  • Users are responsible for securing access to their devices.
  • Data is retained only as long as needed for operational and legal purposes.
  • Backup and recovery practices may be applied by the operating organization.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell users' personal information. Data is only shared when required to operate technical features, comply with legal obligations, or based on the user's explicit request.

Depending on deployment, technical service providers (for example, hosting, storage, analytics, or support tools) may process limited data under contractual safeguards and only for authorized service purposes.

8. International Data Transfers

If data is transferred across jurisdictions, appropriate safeguards are applied as required by applicable regulations.

9. Children

If the app is used in contexts involving children, responsible parties must ensure authorization and supervision according to applicable regulations.

10. User Rights

Users may request correction, update, or deletion of their information, where applicable.

Where required by law, users may also request access, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection to specific uses. Requests may require identity verification.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product improvements or regulatory changes. The "Last updated" date indicates the current version.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the official ChurchDesk support channel.

If you believe your data rights have been violated, you may also contact the competent data protection authority in your jurisdiction where applicable.
