Privacy Policy

Application: ChurchDesk

Last updated: 2026-03-13

This policy explains how ChurchDesk collects, uses, and protects user and device information.

1. Information We Collect

  • User-provided data: member records, notes, and settings entered within the app.
  • Photos: only when the user chooses to select or capture profile images.
  • Technical device data: when applicable, for app functionality, diagnostics, and service improvement.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate and maintain app functionality.
  • Store and display records securely for the user.
  • Generate reports and exports requested by the user.
  • Diagnose issues and improve stability and performance.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera: to capture profile photos when requested by the user.
  • Gallery/Files: to select images or share/export documents.
  • Internet: for network features and content delivery when applicable.

4. Storage and Security

  • Reasonable security measures are applied to protect information.
  • Sensitive data is handled using technical controls aligned with the app architecture.
  • Users are responsible for securing access to their devices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Data is only shared when required to operate technical features, comply with legal obligations, or based on the user's explicit request.

6. Children

If the app is used in contexts involving children, responsible parties must ensure authorization and supervision according to applicable regulations.

7. User Rights

Users may request correction, update, or deletion of their information, where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect product improvements or regulatory changes. The "Last updated" date indicates the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the official ChurchDesk support channel.
